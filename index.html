DEA Scoring Rubric

Care/Challenge — "Did they exude radical candor (care personally, challenge directly)?"
Actionability — "Did they set goals that are actionable and appropriate for the given stakeholder?"

Each scored 1–4, higher is better. The Lead EM assigns a calibrated score to ground-truth each audit. An audit is Calibrated when the auditor's score matches the lead's; otherwise Not Calibrated.

DEA scores feed the Phantom Share Program — high scores can drive double vesting, while sustained underperformance can pause vesting. Calibration is therefore the trust mechanism that keeps the scoring fair across auditors.
